# Benchmark — minimal boutique consultancy websites

> Live-fetch research on **2026-08-02**. Goal: copy patterns to avoid looking like
> "just another consultancy". Verbatim quotes from each site.

## Positive references

### MarsBased — https://marsbased.com/
- H1: "AI-Augmented Development"; sub: "We combine senior engineering talent with a
  proven AI methodology to deliver complex projects on time, on budget, and built to scale."
- Primary steals: **"We take full ownership of the product so development never
  stops. We treat your codebase as if it were ours."** · "Weekly reports, open
  roadmaps, direct communication. You're never left wondering where things stand." ·
  "Simplicity wins." · No account managers: clients talk to the team.
- Proof with numbers, not adjectives: 200+ projects, 12 client exits, 5+ year
  average relationship.

### 37signals — https://37signals.com/
- No hero: the homepage is a numbered index (00–37) of principle titles.
- Declarative titles: "An obligation to independence", "Small teams",
  "Companies aren't families", "Bury the hustle". Zero exclamation marks, almost no CTA.
- Lesson: **titles carry the opinion; the body can be short and factual.**

### thoughtbot — https://thoughtbot.com/
- H1: "When the stakes are high, experience matters"; sub: "Our senior team partners
  with you to deliver reliable software and strengthen your team, even under complex
  constraints." CTA: "Let's talk" (repeated).
- Senior-led in the subhead, not as a badge.

### Rittman Analytics (⭐ gold for data) — https://rittmananalytics.com/
- H1: **"Your data's job has changed."** — 5-word second-person pain-shift.
- Sections: "Your Agents are Ready. Your Data Stack Isn't." · "AI that earns the
  right to be trusted." · **"Outcomes, not deliverables."** · "Built with your
  analysts, not around them".
- Annotated CTA (the highest-leverage steal for a 3-person shop):
  **"Schedule a Discovery Call — 45 minutes · no obligation · you'll speak with a
  founder, not sales"**.
- Delivery model: every sprint has a named engineer as day-to-day contact and the
  CEO as technical sponsor (now in careers/LinkedIn material, not on the home page).
- Names concrete tools (dbt, Looker, BigQuery, Cube) as credentials.

### iA — https://ia.net/
- Functional 5-7 word headlines ("Search to Navigate", "Charts = Tables").
- Lesson: **density comes from deleting sections, not shortening sentences.** 5 sections.

### Basecamp — https://basecamp.com/
- Problem first, second person: "You're juggling people, projects, and expectations."
- Honesty as proof: "Software that's hard to use doesn't get used." · "Service
  isn't an afterthought…" · rhetorical question as credential ("When's the last time
  you praised a tech company's customer service?").

### Simple Thread — https://www.simplethread.com/
- Escalating three-fragment pain-opener: "Power system studies are getting harder.
  More scenarios. More constraints. More pressure to move faster without risking
  reliability." — very stealable rhythm.
- "The goal isn't to replace your engineers. It's to make them more effective."
- A data point in itself: a generalist boutique that rewrote its whole site around
  ONE vertical (energy) in ~2025.

### Test Double — https://testdouble.com/
- H1: "Software problems are human problems."
- Radical price honesty: **"We're not the cheapest, but if you've been burned by
  that we can help you recover."** · "We don't implement AI for its own sake."

## Anti-reference: Thoughtworks ES — https://www.thoughtworks.com/es-es
- "No solo hacemos IA. Hacemos IA que funciona." · "AI/works™ concentra décadas de
  propiedad intelectual…" · "Reconstruir. Reconfigurar. Reimaginar." · "Logremos lo
  extraordinario, junto a ti".
- Fog markers to avoid: trademarked platform names in the hero; abstract nouns
  (transformation, era, extraordinary); re- verb triads with no object; mixed
  ES/EN; claims with no subject doing the work; **nothing falsifiable**.

## Synthesis: 8 applicable patterns (with ES adaptations)

1. **Pain-shift hero**: 4-6 words, second person, a change in the reader's world —
   never what you sell. _"Tu integración ya no aguanta más parches."_
2. **Fog-free subhead**: subject "we", concrete verbs, the actual work.
   _"Diseñamos, construimos y modernizamos plataformas de integración que siguen
   funcionando cuando nadie las mira."_
3. **Annotation under the CTA** (Rittman): boring CTA, differential fine print.
   _"Hablamos 45 minutos. Sin comerciales, porque no tenemos."_ /
   _"Escríbenos. Te responde quien escribirá el código."_
4. **Senior-led as mechanism, not adjective**: _"La persona que lo diseña es la que
   lo entrega. Somos tres. No hay banquillo, no hay rotación, no hay junior
   aprendiendo con tu presupuesto."_
5. **Second-person situation selector** per service: _"Tienes un SAP Commerce que
   nadie del equipo original mantiene."_ + naming concrete tools (dbt, BigQuery,
   Workers, SAP Commerce) — boutiques name tools, consultancies name platforms™.
6. **Declarative titles with a position** (37signals): _"Resultados, no
   entregables."_ · _"Equipos pequeños, sistemas grandes."_ · _"Lo aburrido funciona."_
7. **One radical-honesty line** (the one no consultancy will copy):
   _"No somos los más baratos. Somos tres, y los tres llevan más de quince años en
   producción."_ · _"No hacemos de todo. Hacemos cinco cosas, y las hacemos en
   producción, no en slides."_
8. **Falsifiable proof**: a stat row where the small numbers are the flex —
   _"Desde 2019. 3 personas. 0 comerciales. 0 rondas de inversión."_ Test: if a
   reader can't prove it false, delete it. Minimalism = 5-9 home sections,
   5-15 word headlines.
