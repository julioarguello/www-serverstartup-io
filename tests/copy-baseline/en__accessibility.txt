Accessibility
How to report an accessibility barrier and how quickly we answer.
This is the site's accessibility channel: use it to tell us something here is not accessible, to ask for content in a format that is, or to escalate if what we answer does not help.
How to reach us
Use the contact page and say it is about accessibility. That is enough.
What we do with it
We answer within twenty working days at most. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can escalate and we will reply in writing within two months at most. Those are the deadlines Royal Decree 1112/2018 sets, and we take them as written.
Your data
Whatever you write to us is handled according to the privacy policy.
