CDN, WAF & Edge Security
Server Startup has been an official Cloudflare partner since 2019. We audit, migrate and run CDN, WAF and bot control, mostly for retail chains and e-commerce.
Let's review your Cloudflare
“Imagine an organization’s network as a castle and the network perimeter as a moat. Once the drawbridge is lowered and someone crosses it, they have free rein inside the castle grounds.”

We've worked with the Cloudflare platform since 2017, two years before Server Startup became an official partner. We audit, migrate and tune Enterprise and Business accounts.
Your Cloudflare is configured. Are you sure it's configured right? In many organizations nobody dares touch the WAF rules. They block something. Nobody remembers what.
We know why each rule is there, because we wrote them, we version them with Terraform, and we keep them running in production.
WAF ≠ security
A firewall is one part of security. The other is knowing who changed each rule and what it stopped today. We configure Cloudflare as architecture, with every switch on this panel in Terraform.
